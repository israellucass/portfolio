## 3. About EN

### Hero

- **Eyebrow:** ABOUT ME
- **Title:** Nice to meet you, Ferpa!
- **Subtitle:** Learn about my path and what shapes my professional practice

**Bio**  
My passion for sports and movement started in childhood, and early on I knew I wanted to dedicate my life to helping people through Physical Education.

In 2009 I began my undergraduate studies at the Federal University of Maranhão (UFMA), where I also completed my master's in 2018. I kept going deeper, and in 2024 I earned my PhD in Health Sciences from UFMA in partnership with McGill University (Montreal, Canada).

Since then, I have researched how exercise impacts the human body, from cellular function to public health promotion and disease prevention.

### My story

I live in São Luís (MA), I am married to Beatriz, and I am proud to be part of the LGBTQIAPN+ community. My personal experience helps me build genuine connections with clients seeking welcome, respect, and representation in a judgment-free space. I believe every body deserves care, and that science can be a powerful ally in building a healthier, more balanced life.

### Professional experience

Today I work as a university professor, scientist, and consultant, combining technical knowledge with human sensitivity to offer evidence-based consultancy that promotes autonomy, wellbeing, and quality of life.
