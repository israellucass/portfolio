# Ferpa Lima: stakeholder meeting notes

**Project:** ferpalima.com.br website redesign  
**Stakeholder:** Ferpa Lima (Ferpa Lima Consultoria Fitness)  
**Context:** Discovery / requirements for redesign while keeping platform constraints and conversion goals

---

## Platform constraint

- Keep **Wix** as the website platform.
- Reason: payment processing and finance accountability already run through Wix.
- Implication: design and IA must fit what Wix can support well; explore Wix website-builder capabilities (forms, plans/checkout, SEO settings, CMS/blog, member areas) before proposing flows that need custom engineering.

---

## Brand and positioning

- The site must feel **credible, scientific, and evidence-based**.
- Overall credibility is a hard requirement: no fake promises, no hype-led fitness marketing.
- Core differentiator: **individualized routine** (personalized training plans, not generic templates).
- Content and visual hierarchy should emphasize **exercise science** while still driving **stronger conversions** (clear offer, pricing path, and hire/contact CTAs).

---

## Product focus

| Priority | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Primary story | Exercise science + individualized prescription |
| Trust | Credentials, publications, evidence-based language |
| Conversion | Clear plans, process, and contact / hire paths |
| Avoid | Overpromising outcomes or “miracle” fitness claims |

---

## SEO

- Current SEO is described as **messed up** and needs fixing.
- Follow-up: audit titles, meta descriptions, headings, URL structure, indexing, and Wix SEO settings; align page copy and IA with search intent for consultancy / exercise science / target audiences.

---

## Current audience

- Mainly **women** and **gay men**, roughly **20–45**.
- Strong overlap with the **LGBT** community.
- Motivations: **health** and **weight loss**.
- Emotional need: a **supportive environment** and help overcoming **aversion to fitness** / gym culture.

---

## Target / expansion audiences

Aim to speak more clearly to special groups, including:

- Pregnant clients
- Older adults
- People with diabetes
- Physical fitness test (TAF) preparation
- Chronic pain

These groups should appear in messaging, benefits, and (where relevant) plan / how-it-works framing without diluting the science-led, no-false-promises tone.

---

## Design and content implications (for redesign)

1. Lead with credibility (science, credentials, individualized method) before hard sell.
2. Make individualized routine the hero differentiator on homepage and offer pages.
3. Package conversion (plans, process, contact) as first-class UI, not buried after long biography.
4. Represent inclusive / supportive tone for LGBT and people returning to (or wary of) fitness.
5. Surface special-group coverage (pregnancy, older adults, diabetes, TAF, chronic pain) as clear, scannable signals.
6. Plan SEO remediation within Wix constraints.
7. Validate every proposed pattern against Wix builder limits (especially payments and finance workflows).
